This platform encodes clinical practice guidelines into structured decision-support tools to promote consistency and transparency. These outputs are simplifications of complex recommendations and may not capture all patient-specific factors. SOAP notes require editing to incorporate patient-specific nuance.

Clinical decisions must be made in the context of individual circumstances, clinician expertise, and institutional protocols. Independently verify results and prescriptions. This tool supports, but does not replace, clinical judgment.
