# Adult Asthma Clinical Workflow Revision

Clinical reference review: October 13, 2026. Asset version: `20261013-7`.

This revision is for preview review, not an automatic production promotion. The decision support is deterministic, is intended for adult U.S. pulmonary practice, and does not replace clinician judgment, product labeling, or local emergency protocols. Published guidelines and prescribing information may change after this review.

## Definitive Controller-Plan Selection

- Corrected an AIR-only follow-up edge case: a proposed low-dose SMART step-up could previously have no controller-selection group when technique, adherence, and access remained undocumented. The SOAP note then copied a conditional SMART-versus-Track-2 paragraph. Whenever the engine proposes an initial controller or routine step-up, **Encounter Treatment Plan** now includes the corresponding controller choices. This is a workflow fix, not an automatic prescription.
- Follow-up escalation choices remain unavailable until current optimization is assessed and concerns addressed; the choice controls stay visible with their prerequisites. An inline link opens **Add-ons and treatment optimization**, with instructions to update the review and regenerate. Inadequately controlled AIR-only therapy receives the same optimization-before-step-up safeguard as maintenance treatment. Checking a review does not override positively documented adherence, technique, or access concerns. [GINA 2026 Summary Guide, treatment adjustment](https://ginasthma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/GINA-Summary-Guide-2026-WEB-WMS.pdf).
- Selecting SMART documents its direct start/change instruction and existing product-specific schedule. Selecting Track 2 documents a direct start/change instruction, a controller prescription placeholder, and only the chosen compatible reliever. Replaced controllers are not duplicated. Removed the conditional step-up paragraph from the fallback note template; an unresolved controller decision remains a focused `***` placeholder instead of competing alternatives. Selection does not alter the recorded current regimen or invent completed treatment or safety review.
- Verification: 158 clinical/note/site tests, including AIR-only with incomplete optimization, supported SMART and Track 2 selections, blocked selections, changed prerequisites, matching medication details, and fallback wording. Browser coverage includes keyboard selection, review navigation, automatic note updates, clipboard/stale safeguards, green generation confirmation, preserved regeneration position, and 320/390/768/1280 px layouts. No prediction API, persistence, backend, or production changes.

## Symptom-Control Completion and Regeneration Feedback

- Changed activity limitation from a positive-only checkbox to explicit **Not assessed / Yes / No** choices beside daytime symptoms and night waking. Previously, an unmarked checkbox remained unknown, so an apparently completed encounter could still have incomplete symptom control. The negative-symptom shortcut still records an intentional negative answer. Removed the duplicate activity-limitation question from the post-generation confirmations.
- Shared control findings now identify the exact unanswered fields. The input summary names missing answers, and Assessment Summary provides direct links to them. These interface reminders are not appended to the copied SOAP note. An unknown reliever still prevents treatment-aware completion; documented **No reliever** is no longer mistaken for missing history. No-reliever / no-ICS treatment risks and ICS-containing treatment recommendations remain effective independently of symptom control. No SABA-frequency answer is required for an ICS-containing reliever or a documented absence of a reliever. The symptom thresholds, explicit-negative safeguards, and priority of known uncontrolled symptoms remain intact. [GINA 2026 Summary Guide, Table 2, symptom control and separate exacerbation risk](https://ginasthma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/GINA-Summary-Guide-2026-WEB-WMS.pdf).
- Successful generation or regeneration displays a green checkmark on the submitted button, with an accessible current-results description and live confirmation. The icon's space is reserved to avoid layout shift. Clinical edits clear the indicator and retain the existing stale-output / clipboard safeguards. Invalid output and failed generation never receive a success indicator. These are usability changes, not assurances of clinical safety.
- Generation no longer scrolls to the results, moves focus to the results heading, or automatically navigates to invalid entries. The clinician's viewport and button focus are preserved, while explicit error links still allow navigation. Open individual-confirmation drawers retain reviewed answers and their regeneration button after completion; their answers continue to reflect shared clinical state and can be revised.
- Verification: 153 clinical/note/site regression tests; browser scenarios for complete and incomplete history, reliever-specific missing answers, no-reliever encounters, successful and failed/urgent generation, all three generation buttons with stable viewport/focus, clearing indicators after edits, dosing and plan selections, clipboard, keyboard use, and 320/390/768/1280 px layouts. No persistence, backend, API, or production changes.

## Explicit Inhaled-Regimen Plus Biologic Plan

- Added **Continue current inhaled regimen and initiate a phenotype-matched biologic** as an explicit encounter-plan choice for appropriate poorly controlled Step 4-5 presentations. Selection foregrounds agent choices and produces a unified plan using the current inhaled regimen and the selected eligible agent; it does not replace ICS treatment or introduce higher SMART dosing.
- Removed the **Adequate trial of optimized treatment completed** and **Severe asthma previously confirmed** checkboxes and their state/schema keys. The treatment-history examples and eligibility instructions now live in **Encounter Treatment Plan**, shown only when biologic matching is relevant. Selecting initiation explicitly represents the clinician's review of severe asthma and the adequate optimized ICS-LABA treatment history. Merely entering a high ICS dose, checking the treatment/barrier review, or matching biomarkers does not establish completed longitudinal review or initiation readiness. This is a documentation/workflow change, not a relaxation of GINA severe-asthma requirements. [GINA 2026 Severe Asthma Guide, definitions and biologic eligibility](https://ginasthma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GINA-Severe-Asthma-Guide-2026-WEB-WMS.pdf), [GINA 2026 Summary Guide, Step 5 and Table 7](https://ginasthma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/GINA-Summary-Guide-2026-WEB-WMS.pdf).
- Instructions ask clinicians to verify diagnosis, technique, adherence, access, contributors, treatment history, phenotype, exacerbations, product requirements, dosing, and coverage. They explain inadequate control despite an adequate optimized high-dose ICS-LABA assessment or a documented longitudinal severe-asthma diagnosis, including worsening when high-dose treatment was reduced. Selecting the plan never populates negative findings, completed administration, or inferred high-dose exposure.
- Numeric, diagnostic, phenotype, pregnancy, compatibility, existing-treatment, and drug-specific safeguards remain independently evaluated. Missing prerequisites disable the corresponding initiation choice while retaining **Continue inhaled regimen and complete biologic assessment**. If a previously selected initiation plan becomes unsupported, the shared builder downgrades to assessment with an explicit safety message and recomputes readiness and note wording. Suspected EGPA requires diagnostic/organ-involvement assessment before asthma-only biologic initiation; confirmed disease retains indication-specific dosing.
- Existing biologic treatment offers response and coordinated single-agent review, not addition of a second biologic. Active exacerbations and urgent findings suppress elective biologic initiation regardless of stored selections. Choosing an agent alone leaves initiation conditional until the combined clinical plan is selected. Missing omalizumab/reslizumab dosing information retains discussion options but prevents a definite dose.
- With initiation selected, the note contains continuation of the current inhaled regimen, the chosen ready biologic, its verified dosing, and monitoring. If no agent is chosen, it reserves `Biologic agent and dosing: ***`. Unselected LAMA and azithromycin alternatives are not copied as competing immediate plans. An explicitly selected compatible LAMA add-on can coexist with the biologic plan. An unresolved initiation decision uses a placeholder rather than assuming an otherwise suitable patient must wait until follow-up. Care recommendations do not assert medication administration or completed counseling.
- Verification: 151 pure logic/note/site tests and browser checks at 320, 390, 768, and 1280 pixels. Scenarios include removed controls, instructions at plan selection, medium MART with biologic initiation, agent selection without initiation, missing/changed prerequisites, dosing safeguards, assessment downgrade, current-biologic review, retained urgent priority, clipboard/stale behavior, and keyboard access. No persistence, backend, API, or production changes.

## Selected Encounter Plans and Step 4-5 Refinement

- Retained exactly five current-inhaler shortcuts: None, as-needed SABA, AIR-only, low-dose SMART, and medium-dose SMART. Removed the high-dose ICS-LABA shortcut and its specific helper text, not the detailed high-dose input or drug reference. Shortcuts remain nonprescriptive and preserve add-ons, phenotype, symptoms, and other findings. This is a usability choice.
- Removed the symptoms/risk review flag and its blanket negative defaults. Positive-only risk checkboxes remain undocumented when unmarked; blank counts remain unknown. The symptom-negative and zero-exacerbation shortcuts still document deliberate assessments. Targeted individual confirmations appear only where control, step-down, biologic planning, or cessation prescribing needs them. A confirmation changes clinical state, marks results stale, and requires regeneration. Removing or checking a previously confirmed exception restores the appropriate unknown/positive state.
- Added a shared pure treatment-plan layer (`plans.js`). Clinicians can select an appropriate controller strategy, a compatible separate reliever where needed, LAMA add-on or deferral, and eligible supervised step-down. Existing biologic and cessation selections coexist. Selected actions and medication references update displayed recommendations and the SOAP note together. Unresolved decisions use focused `***` placeholders without disabling copying. Invalidated choices are cleared with an explanatory safety message, not silently reused.
- Corrected the distinction between ICS intensity and Step 5 add-on treatment. Budesonide-formoterol 160/4.5 mcg, 2 inhalations twice daily plus 1 as needed remains the adult Step 4-5 MART schedule; LAMA/biologic/maintenance-OCS add-ons do not relabel it high-dose ICS. A supported high-dose ICS-LABA trial remains an option rather than a required controller switch before every Step 5 intervention. No higher scheduled SMART regimen was introduced. [GINA 2026 Summary Guide, Step 5 and Table 7](https://ginasthma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/GINA-Summary-Guide-2026-WEB-WMS.pdf).
- Recognize clinician-confirmed longitudinal severe asthma while retaining supported diagnosis, an appropriate current ICS-LABA foundation, current optimization, disease burden, and agent-specific prerequisites. The prior confirmation represents an established assessment of inadequate response to optimized high-dose treatment or worsening after its reduction; it is not inferred from medium MART failure. It prevents an unnecessary repeat high-dose trial from being imposed solely because today's regimen is medium MART. New biologic treatment remains provisional with missing diagnostic, phenotype, safety, dosing, pregnancy, exacerbation, or product/payer information. This is a documentation/workflow interpretation of the longitudinal severe-asthma definition, not a relaxation of product or payer eligibility. [GINA 2026 Severe Asthma Guide, definition, optimization, and biologic selection](https://ginasthma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GINA-Severe-Asthma-Guide-2026-WEB-WMS.pdf).
- Step-down now requires an individual assessment of prior life-threatening asthma as well as the existing stability and safety checks. Prior life-threatening history is not an automatic prohibition, but requires close supervision. Patients with Step 5 add-ons or established severe asthma retain individualized add-on/OCS review rather than receiving the routine uncomplicated MART reduction shortcut. Follow-up wording matches the selected reduction plan.
- Added an EGPA reminder for eosinophilic asthma/sinus disease with neuropathy, purpura, unexplained infiltrates, or systemic organ involvement. Eosinophilia alone is not confirmation and negative ANCA does not exclude disease. This is reference text, not a new diagnostic score. [2023 EGPA guideline](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41584-023-00958-w), [2020 ANCA consensus](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32584187/).
- Consolidated cessation safety review: seizure history is collected once and drives bupropion contraindication and varenicline precaution separately. The other-bupropion-contraindication flag excludes that duplicate question. Psychiatric concerns refer to bipolar/mania or active instability, not stable depression or psychiatric history alone. Ready chosen plans document a direct course; missing prerequisites produce concrete assessment tasks and a final-dose placeholder. [Bupropion SR label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=a591e33a-52f0-41c8-a00b-4a1afcc3dc4c), [ATS cessation guidance](https://doi.org/10.1164/rccm.202005-1982ST).
- SOAP plans use direct clinical actions and only chosen treatment pathways. Present biologic recommendations remain distinct from conditional follow-up planning. Dosing is copied only for a ready chosen biologic; original omalizumab table and reslizumab weight safeguards remain unchanged. No completed counseling, testing, vaccination, administration, transfer, or normal findings are invented. Existing Subjective/Objective/Assessment/Plan headings and documentation placeholders remain.
- Verification: 140 pure logic/note/site tests plus browser scenarios at 320, 390, 768, and 1280 pixels, including actual individual-negative confirmations, simultaneous controller/LAMA/biologic selections, compatible relievers, selection invalidation, keyboard access, clipboard, and stale output. No persistence, prediction API, backend, or production changes.

## Treatment Choices and Encounter Documentation

- The form now starts in **Routine visit**, with the previously requested No red-flag default. These are visible workflow defaults, not objective acute-safety verification. Explicit unknown/positive answers and known urgent measurements remain effective. Moving from routine to active/resolved context clears only the automatic red-flag negative.
- Added **High-Dose ICS-LABA** as a safe current-treatment shortcut, with a non-formoterol LABA and compatible SABA reliever. This intentionally is not labelled high-dose SMART: GINA's adult budesonide-formoterol 160/4.5 schedule for Steps 4-5 MART is 2 inhalations twice daily plus as-needed use, not a higher scheduled SMART dose. The clinician can change the reliever/category to match the actual regimen; no other findings are reset. [GINA 2026 Summary Guide, Table 7](https://ginasthma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/GINA-Summary-Guide-2026-WEB-WMS.pdf), [U.S. Symbicort labeling](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=fafa4cf1-99c2-43d5-73ad-51f256de3be0).
- Current smoking reveals a collapsed, optional dosing/safety review and a dedicated cessation section. Varenicline, combination patch plus short-acting nicotine gum, bupropion SR, and behavioral support are selectable. Evidence, comparative benefits, comorbidity considerations, contraindications, and standard/renal-specific courses are expandable. Only the preferred plan enters the SOAP note. Safety or dosing uncertainty keeps pharmacotherapy conditional; documented bupropion contraindications or a serious prior varenicline reaction block the corresponding option. Pregnancy/breastfeeding, seizure concerns, renal/hepatic impairment, and unstable cardiovascular conditions require appropriate individual review rather than generic clearance. No counseling, medication administration, refusal, or actual prescription is inferred.
- Cessation efficacy is described qualitatively from [ATS pharmacotherapy guidance](https://doi.org/10.1164/rccm.202005-1982ST) and the [WHO 2024 evidence synthesis](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK604665/): varenicline outperforms a single patch or bupropion, combination NRT outperforms single NRT, and evidence does not establish a clear varenicline advantage over combination NRT. Behavioral support accompanies medication. [USPSTF pregnancy guidance](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/tobacco-use-in-adults-and-pregnant-women-counseling-and-interventions) supports behavioral intervention first in pregnancy. Doses and precautions follow [varenicline labeling](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=16a03b5e-03e0-4b0b-92a4-aaf0669906f7), [bupropion SR cessation labeling](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=a591e33a-52f0-41c8-a00b-4a1afcc3dc4c), [CDC patch](https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/campaign/tips/quit-smoking/quit-smoking-medications/how-to-use-quit-smoking-medicines/how-to-use-a-nicotine-patch.html), and [CDC gum guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/campaign/tips/quit-smoking/quit-smoking-medications/how-to-use-quit-smoking-medicines/how-to-use-nicotine-gum.html). A practical review 1-2 weeks after a quit attempt is a workflow choice, not a universal guideline mandate.
- Assessment prose is composed from structured findings in complete sentences without semicolons, colons, or hyphens. Age, diagnosis, encounter context, current therapy, reliever, symptom control, exacerbation risk, and phenotype remain distinct facts. Missing data are not converted into normal findings.
- Each phenotype-matched biologic now includes concise advantages, limitations, typical prescribing criteria, and an explicit patient-fit assessment. Thresholds described as common eligibility or trial signals are not universal FDA/payer rules. Comparisons are not head-to-head efficacy rankings. Details use [GINA severe-asthma guidance](https://ginasthma.org/2026-gina-severe-asthma-guide/) and the existing linked U.S. product references, including [depemokimab trial/label information](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=30332b20-2ac0-42ad-a775-d3ca7f5fe29f).
- If documented severity, optimization, phenotype, and agent prerequisites support current therapy, the note says the chosen biologic **has been recommended**, without claiming it was administered or prescribed. Otherwise, subsequent follow-up after an appropriate optimized high-dose ICS-LABA trial, or completion of outstanding testing, remains explicitly conditional. Existing biologic treatment requires response/switch review, not automatic dual therapy. Monitoring is timed from actual future initiation. Generic and agent-specific plan statements are consolidated.
- An unsupported high-dose SMART description cannot establish optimized high-dose exposure or current severe-asthma eligibility. It and an incompatible LABA controller/reliever combination add biologic prescribing prerequisites. Standard medium-dose SMART cannot be turned into verified high-dose exposure by selecting a high-dose label. Tests of immediate biologic recommendations use a supported high-dose ICS-LABA controller with a compatible reliever.
- Smoking and biologic selections use the same immutable findings and can coexist without resetting each other. Input changes disable both selectors and copying until regeneration. A provisional cessation choice leaves a definitive prescription and dose pending in the note instead of copying a potentially unsuitable generic course; standard references remain accessible. No persistence or API was added; urgent care still suppresses routine biologic and cessation recommendations. Verification includes cessation courses/contraindications, selection/stale/copy behavior, biologic timing and fit, Assessment punctuation, and responsive layouts. Delivery remains preview-only.

## Clinician Form and Vaccination Refinements

- Removed the two repetitive daily-limit / U.S.-prescribing paragraphs beneath the AIR/SMART input table. The table and source links remain; product-specific limits, off-label status, spacer guidance, and incompatible-LABA safeguards remain in generated medication guidance and the SOAP medication plan.
- Defaulted current biologic to **None** as requested. Explicitly changing it to an agent or **Not assessed** is preserved, including after treatment-group confirmation. This is a pragmatic input default, not a guideline inference.
- Selecting **Routine visit** defaults an unanswered red-flag field to **No**, but never overwrites a positive finding or suppresses urgent observations. Changing to another encounter context clears only the automatic default; clinician-entered responses remain intact. The note says no red-flag features were **documented**, not that the patient denied them. This is a user-requested workflow assumption, not verification of acute safety.
- Organized add-ons into separate maintenance-therapy, biologic, barrier, and treatment-readiness panels. Helper text sits outside checkbox labels, and the panels stack on mobile. Renamed the optimization confirmation to **Adequate trial of optimized treatment completed**, with its longitudinal requirements explained. Added hypothetical prior-severe-asthma examples; these do not enter the note or change severe-asthma prerequisites. [GINA 2026 severe-asthma guide, definition and optimization review](https://ginasthma.org/2026-gina-severe-asthma-guide/).
- Removed blanket phenotype-group confirmation. Allergen-driven symptoms, polyps, dermatitis, and parasite exposure now have individual **Yes / No / Not assessed** choices. Sensitization, EGPA, smoking, and biomarkers retain their independent assessment; blank values remain unknown.
- A confirmed **Due / incomplete** vaccine status now generates actionable product / schedule guidance in both recommendations and SOAP from one shared action. Unknown history continues to prompt record review, and **Current / complete** suppresses further doses. Unverified age/risk eligibility prompts reconciliation instead of an unsupported vaccination order. Pregnancy and previous-dose cautions remain conditional; no administered vaccination is inferred. [CDC pneumococcal](https://www.cdc.gov/pneumococcal/hcp/vaccine-recommendations/index.html), [RSV](https://www.cdc.gov/rsv/hcp/vaccine-clinical-guidance/adults.html), [zoster](https://www.cdc.gov/shingles/hcp/vaccine-considerations/index.html), [Tdap / adult notes](https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-schedules/adult-notes.html).
- Verified influenza's annual schedule / preferred older-adult formulations and the CDC's September 2026 adult COVID-19 update. COVID-19 advice uses the current seasonal, age-dependent schedule, not the older COVID block in the adult schedule notes; immune status and previous doses must be verified. [CDC influenza guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/flu/hcp/acip/), [CDC 2026-2027 COVID-19 guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/covid/hcp/vaccine-considerations/routine-guidance.html).
- Regression coverage includes defaults and reversal, individually assessed phenotype, preserved urgency, shared vaccine actions/notes, vaccine age boundaries, prior-series safeguards, pregnancy, stale/copy behavior, and responsive add-on/phenotype layouts. No persistence, backend, or production promotion was introduced.

## Point-of-Care Usability Refinements

- Added **None** (no ICS controller / reliever) and **As-needed SABA** (SABA reliever without an ICS controller) as current-regimen shortcuts. Like AIR/SMART shortcuts, they change only controller, reliever, ICS intensity, and LABA context. Other findings, add-ons, biologics, OCS exposure, counts, and urgent observations remain intact. Neither selection is an endorsement of absent ICS protection; the existing ICS-containing treatment recommendations remain in force. [GINA 2026, SABA-only treatment](https://ginasthma.org/2026-gina-strategy-report/).
- Redesigned the five shortcuts as readable regimen tiles with short descriptions and an accessible pressed state. The highlight follows manual controller/reliever/intensity changes; it indicates the recorded category, not verification of an exact product or prescription. Reapplying an unchanged shortcut preserves the current note, while actual changes still invalidate it.
- Converted daytime symptoms, night waking, SABA frequency, and exacerbation context to directly selectable options. Every prior value and explicit **Not assessed** state is retained. No additional negatives or zero counts are inferred. Normalized radio sizing corrects oversized circles while keeping labelled touch targets at least 44 px high.
- Added section links for treatment, diagnosis, symptoms, acute care, biomarkers, and vaccines. Links open optional sections when needed and move keyboard focus without changing inputs or invalidating the note. Generated results include a direct SOAP-note link, hidden when the output is stale or invalid.
- Preserved the established navy/serif visual identity, strengthened selected-state contrast, and organized the controls into responsive grids. Clinical thresholds, treatment actions, medication schedules, and copy/stale safeguards are unchanged. The note now describes a confirmed absence of controller/reliever clearly rather than printing "the reliever was no reliever."
- Added regression checks for the new presets, retained urgent priority and incomplete history, keyboard use, matched highlights, disclosure/navigation without note changes, accessible tap targets, and layouts at 320/390/768/1280 px. Delivery remains preview-only.

The table distinguishes no scheduled maintenance, 1 inhalation twice daily, and 2 inhalations twice daily; reliever use, the shared 12-inhalation daily limit, U.S. off-label status, and LABA compatibility are explicit. ## Streamlined Workflow Revision

- Removed free-text encounter fields and routine BP, heart rate, temperature, weight, RR, and SpO2 entry. The SOAP draft reserves three `***` placeholders for interval history, current medications/strengths/schedule, and examination. Active exacerbations add acute-history and administered-treatment placeholders; neither placeholder asserts that treatment occurred.
- Added optional AIR-only, low-dose SMART, and medium-dose SMART shortcuts. These populate only current controller, ICS intensity, LABA context, and reliever; they preserve the other findings and do not prescribe or reset add-ons.
- Treatment/barrier, risk/stability, and phenotype groups use positive exception checkboxes. A group's separate review confirmation resolves its unchecked routine exceptions to absent/without concern. Without confirmation they remain unknown, and checked positives always remain effective. Reversing a group confirmation restores unknown defaults. Blank counts are never zero. Diagnostic evidence, sensitization, pretreatment IgE, optimization over time, stable lung function, and acute safety remain independent assessments. This is a workflow safeguard, not a guideline-defined checklist.
- Omalizumab/biosimilar and reslizumab remain supported options under **Additional Biologic Options**. Selecting either reveals optional verified weight; omalizumab also reveals pretreatment IgE and timing confirmation. Missing dosing data leaves the option provisional rather than hiding it. The omalizumab adult asthma table and sensitization safeguards are unchanged. Reslizumab remains 3 mg/kg IV every 4 weeks; a patient-specific mg amount requires verified weight. [U.S. Cinqair prescribing information](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=053b9158-2a5b-48b9-bf47-5fa78a35ec33).
- Initial acute observations are requested only for an active episode; actual repeat observations require a separate reassessment-available confirmation. A resolved recent exacerbation activates routine recovery/risk review, not discharge assessment. Previously recorded urgent findings remain visible and retain priority even if encounter context changes. Missing acute observations cannot establish mild severity or discharge readiness. GINA severity thresholds are unchanged.
- SOAP history, testing, and assessment use retrospective facts. Plan wording is derived from structured actions (for example, "Recommended continuation" and "Planned reassessment"), not automatic tense substitution. Conditional escalation and future biologic therapy remain conditional. The editable-draft notice is outside the copied text. Drug-specific dosing edits invalidate the note and require regeneration through the same clinical state.
- Clarified standalone ICS and leukotriene modifiers (such as montelukast) can continue for bronchodilator-response testing. Combination inhalers follow the bronchodilator interval; bronchoprovocation follows a separate lab protocol. [ATS/ERS spirometry standard](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6794117/).
- Added a collapsible CDC vaccine quick reference: pneumococcal at 19-49 with asthma / all adults at least 50; one-dose RSV at 50-74 with asthma / all adults at least 75; zoster at least 50 or immunocompromised at least 19; Tdap/Td primary series and 10-year boosters. Products, prior-vaccine/immune-status adjustments, pregnancy, and wound-care caveats are included. The desktop table becomes labelled stacked rows on mobile; reference text is not copied into the note. [CDC pneumococcal guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/pneumococcal/hcp/vaccine-recommendations/index.html), [RSV guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/rsv/hcp/vaccine-clinical-guidance/adults.html), [zoster guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/shingles/hcp/vaccine-considerations/index.html), [adult notes](https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-schedules/adult-notes.html).
- Matched copied follow-up wording to the existing displayed guidance for patients with frequent or persistent exacerbations: an earlier practical 4-6-week post-adjustment review is considered despite low current symptom burden. This interval is a pragmatic outpatient choice, not a universal guideline requirement.
- No backend or persistence was introduced. Production remains unchanged pending clinical preview review.

## Pragmatic Choices and Limits

- FEV1 below 80% predicted is a practical signal of reduced lung function during initial step selection, not a GINA diagnostic threshold.
- Eosinophils of at least 150 currently or 300 historically and FeNO of at least 25 are phenotype signals, not universal FDA or payer eligibility cutoffs. For the risk display, eosinophils at least 300 or FeNO at least 50 flag raised markers; treatment selection still uses the full context.
- A measured respiratory rate above 20/min conservatively prevents the mild acute label even if respiratory effort was described as normal/mild. The GINA severe boundary remains above 30/min; qualitative findings and the worst feature still apply.
- Missing optimization details produce conditional, not unconditional, escalation. Missing step-down prerequisites retain current ICS treatment. A prior life-threatening event requires closer supervision, not an invented universal prohibition on step-down.
- Omalizumab table timing, optimization, and medication selection are clinician confirmations. Medication details are documented in the EMR placeholder; they do not assign dose intensity or calculate a prescription.
- Biologic matching is not a head-to-head effectiveness ranking. Authorization, clinical eligibility, hypersensitivity precautions, current response, and shared decision-making still require review before treatment.
- For a chosen dupilumab option, the note proposes the FDA-supported 400 mg loading / 200 mg every-2-week asthma schedule unless OCS dependence or a qualifying documented AD/CRSwNP comorbidity supports 600 mg loading / 300 mg every 2 weeks. The clinician must verify the final prescription and concurrent indications.
- Routine treatment-change follow-up of approximately 4-6 weeks and step-down review in 4-8 weeks are practical outpatient intervals; urgent need overrides these intervals.
- The acute pathway supports initial assessment and outpatient reassessment, not resuscitation/ICU management. It does not replace direct assessment or local transfer criteria.
- Severe measured hypoxemia or tachypnea triggers urgent assessment even when acute mode is off. This guard does not infer that the cause is asthma. Post-treatment room-air oxygen conditions are collected independently; incomplete baseline assessment or missing repeat respiratory rate cannot establish the disposition criteria.
- Encounter data live only in page memory. The asthma tool does not save patient inputs to local storage or send them to a prediction API. Existing site analytics is unchanged and receives no form values from this application.

Set `ASTHMA_TEST_URL` to test a preview URL; `PLAYWRIGHT_CHANNEL=chrome` uses an existing Chrome installation. These development tools are not loaded by the calculator.

Use synthetic data only when reviewing the preview:

1. Newly evaluated adult with typical symptoms and exact diagnostic bronchodilator cutoffs; compare the distinct ATS/ERS result.
2. Established asthma with unavailable historical report and partial symptom / exacerbation history; verify there is no false reassurance or automatic invalidation.
3. Stable medium-dose SMART or effective Track 2 treatment; document all step-down prerequisites and then change one to unknown or positive.
4. Poor control on medium-dose SMART with eosinophils / FeNO entered; inspect conditional future biologic planning rather than immediate initiation.
5. Persistent burden on optimized high-dose ICS-LABA with complete exacerbation history; compare supported options and choose one for the note.
6. Eosinophils 1499, 1500, and 1501; review the separate evidence caution and hypereosinophilia workup.
7. Omalizumab with supported and unsupported IgE/weight cells, missing sensitization, or undocumented pretreatment timing.
8. Mild, moderate, severe, and incomplete acute presentations; document actual treatment / response, and then enter deterioration to verify urgent priority.
9. Change an input after generation; verify copy and biologic selection remain disabled until regeneration. Inspect all SOAP facts against the inputs.
