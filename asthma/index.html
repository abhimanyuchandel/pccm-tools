PneumococcalAdults 19-49 with asthma; all adults 50 or older.PCV20 (Prevnar 20) or PCV21 (Capvaxive) alone, or PCV15 (Vaxneuvance) followed ordinarily 1 year later by PPSV23 (Pneumovax 23). Previous vaccination changes the schedule; not an annual vaccine. CDC pneumococcal guidance

Tdap / TdAdults according to primary-series and prior Tdap history; not asthma-specific.Tdap when indicated, then Td or Tdap every 10 years. Incomplete primary series: Tdap, Td/Tdap at least 4 weeks later, then Td/Tdap 6-12 months later. Tdap during each pregnancy, preferably at 27-36 weeks. CDC adult schedule

Pneumococcal intervals may shorten to 8 weeks for qualifying immunocompromise, CSF leak, or cochlear implant, not asthma alone. Wound-care tetanus schedules differ. Maternal RSV prevention uses Abrysvo at 32-36 weeks under seasonal obstetric guidance. For age 18, consult the CDC adolescent schedule. Do not restart a vaccine series solely because an interval was prolonged.
