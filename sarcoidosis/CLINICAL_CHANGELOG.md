# Sarcoidosis Clinical Rules and Sources

Evidence review: October 16, 2026. Initial clinical-review preview; not independently validated.

## Evidence Hierarchy and Scope

This adult U.S. pulmonary-led follow-up tool distinguishes guideline recommendations, trial findings, expert practice and conservative implementation safeguards. It is not an organ-threatening rescue pathway, infusion order set, electronic prescribing system, interaction database or transplant-listing tool. No investigational therapy is offered.

| Source | Application |
| --- | --- |
| [ATS diagnosis/detection, 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7159433/) | Compatible presentation, tissue when needed and context-specific exclusion of alternatives; established/classic nonbiopsy diagnoses are retained. Baseline eye examination, ECG, CBC, calcium, creatinine and alkaline phosphatase. Annual calcium/creatinine/alkaline-phosphatase review after negative baseline assessment. Symptom-triggered additional ocular/cardiac testing rather than automatic repeated advanced imaging. |
| [ERS treatment guideline, 2021](https://publications.ersnet.org/lookup/pmid/34140301) | Treat to reduce organ risk or unacceptable quality-of-life burden; glucocorticoids, methotrexate and refractory-disease infliximab pathways. No automatic immunosuppression from a radiographic stage, biomarker or PFT alone. |
| [BTS clinical statement](https://thorax.bmj.com/content/76/1/4) | Expert-practice guidance for pulmonary assessment, longitudinal physiology, symptoms, tapering and follow-up. PFT changes require context and comparability. |
| [PREDMETH, 2025](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2501443) | Methotrexate was noninferior to prednisone for change in percent-predicted FVC at 24 weeks in selected treatment-naive pulmonary patients. This does not establish equivalent speed, all-organ efficacy, superiority in adverse-event frequency, or suitability for urgent organ-threatening disease. |
| [SARCORT, 2023](https://publications.ersnet.org/content/erj/62/3/2300198.full) | A 40 mg starting prednisolone regimen was not superior to 20 mg for relapse/treatment failure in the studied population. This is not a prohibition on individualized higher-dose organ-specific care. |
| [Infliximab pulmonary RCT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16840744/) | Modest mean FVC improvement in chronic refractory pulmonary disease; does not guarantee individual response. |
| [Adalimumab pulmonary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4134103/) | Smaller open-label evidence; not presented as equal first-line evidence. No automatic uveitis/IBD loading schedule. |
| [Practical pharmacology, Kwon/Judson, 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10932410/) | Expert dosing, titration, interactions, slower response of conventional steroid-sparing agents, laboratory monitoring and comparative limitations. Dose templates are not equivalent to FDA approval for sarcoidosis. |
| [Endocrine Society/ESE, 2024](https://academic.oup.com/jcem/article/109/7/1657/7667842) | Consider exposure, withdrawal and adrenal suppression during reduction, particularly near physiologic doses. Routine taper templates do not establish HPA-axis recovery. |
| [AAO 2025 revision, published 2026](https://www.aaojournal.org/article/S0161-6420%2825%2900709-2/fulltext) | HCQ weight limit and baseline OCT/FAF; annual surveillance with risk-dependent deferral during the first 5 years. Separate from ocular sarcoidosis assessment. |
| [CDC adult schedule](https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-schedules/adult-notes.html), [RSV](https://www.cdc.gov/rsv/hcp/vaccine-clinical-guidance/adults.html), [pneumococcal](https://www.cdc.gov/pneumococcal/hcp/vaccine-recommendations/index.html), [immune compromise](https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-best-practices/altered-immunocompetence.html) | Age/risk/product/previous-dose vaccine reminders, immune-status distinctions and live-vaccine precautions. No administration is asserted. No automatic medication interruption. |
| [ACR glucocorticoid bone guidance](https://rheumatology.org/glucocorticoid-induced-osteoporosis-guideline), [EULAR infection prevention](https://ard.bmj.com/content/82/6/742) | Bone/steroid review and individualized PJP assessment. PJP thresholds are extrapolated from other inflammatory diseases, not declared a universal sarcoidosis rule. Calcium/vitamin-D supplementation is not automatic. |
| [WASOG SAPH statement](https://publications.ersnet.org/lookup/doi/10.1183/16000617.0165-2021) | PH suspicion/workup and hemodynamic confirmation when indicated. No automatic PAH drug or transplant eligibility declaration. |
| [Endotext hypercalcemia](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK279037/) | Severe or symptomatic calcium findings trigger urgent assessment; no albumin correction or acute treatment regimen is inferred. |

## Prescribing Templates and Safeguards

- Prednisone: editable 5-40 mg/day pulmonary template, usually initialized at 20 mg, with a 1-6-week reassessment period. This is not a cardiac/neurologic rescue regimen.
- Methotrexate: editable 5-25 mg once weekly, usually 15 mg toward 25 mg with increases up to 5 mg every 4 weeks; oral or subcutaneous route and folic acid 1 mg/day. The standard template conservatively requires eGFR at least 60. Lower renal function does not imply methotrexate is universally contraindicated; it requires individualized dose adjustment outside this template. Daily current MTX is a blocking medication-error warning. [U.S. label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=948e0bab-6524-4b87-9853-1799d4415558).
- Azathioprine: usually 50 mg/day, editable 25 mg increments to a selected target no greater than 250 mg/day. Standard template requires suitable TPMT/NUDT15 review and no xanthine-oxidase inhibitor. Reduced metabolism or allopurinol needs individualized dosing; febuxostat is not recommended. CBC monitoring follows the more frequent U.S. label initiation schedule; genetic testing never replaces CBC monitoring. [U.S. label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=4d62b6e4-a9d4-4818-e063-6394a90aa208).
- Leflunomide: 10-20 mg/day without a loading dose. Explicit hepatic, reproductive and TB review. Avoid routine MTX duplication; drug elimination/washout must be considered when appropriate. [U.S. label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=91712aad-d96c-4ef0-97e7-b31ed18ca6d8).
- Mycophenolate mofetil: usually 500 mg twice daily toward 1,000 mg twice daily, with editable 250 mg steps and maximum template target 1,500 mg twice daily. Do not interchange with mycophenolate sodium milligram-for-milligram. Reproductive safety and monitoring follow labeling/local protocol. [U.S. label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=721564bc-7c0b-4418-ab49-0af362e74eb7).
- Hydroxychloroquine: 200 or 400 mg/day only when actual weight permits at most 5 mg/kg/day, ordinarily no more than 400 mg/day. A lower individualized schedule may be needed. Not a substitute for significant active pulmonary disease. Retinal and cardiac/QT review are explicit. [U.S. label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=e5fe2ece-40a1-49eb-818f-62ff3c3bb199).
- Infliximab: 3 or 5 mg/kg at weeks 0/2/6 for selected new initiation, then every 4/6/8 weeks; established maintenance does not re-induce automatically. Weight-derived amount is before pharmacy verification/rounding. Routine template does not start TNF treatment with known HF; this is more conservative than the label's specific contraindication for doses above 5 mg/kg in moderate/severe HF. [U.S. label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=b05c174f-832c-4321-b34f-2c4ad3742269).
- Adalimumab: 40 mg every 1 or 2 weeks, no automatic loading regimen. Product and device must be verified. [U.S. label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=608d4f0d-b19f-46d3-749a-7159aa5f933d).
- New prescriptions require drug-specific review. Missing prerequisites generate a testing/review plan and placeholder, not a definitive dose. Current prescriptions remain separate from proposed changes. Standard plans avoid second conventional agents, overlapping TNF agents and automatic TMP-SMX with MTX.
- Starting a new agent while background treatment exists requires explicit retention/replacement selections. Retained conventional/TNF treatment keeps its monitoring actions. Prednisone and prednisolone history remain distinct; unidentified steroid products are documented using verified prednisone-equivalent doses, not guessed product-specific dosing.
- Staged prednisone reduction requires actual dose/exposure and explicit stability/adrenal review. The routine template does not taper prolonged exposure to zero; individualized cessation remains available in clinical practice outside the template.
- Exposure before a proposed zero-dose stage includes the planned taper as well as previous treatment. A conservative three-week review boundary is used to avoid an unverified long-course cessation plan; this is an implementation safeguard, not proof that all shorter courses have zero adrenal risk.

## Workflow and Documentation Choices

- Clinical entries remain in page memory only. No backend, storage, query-string serialization or patient-data requests are added.
- Unknown consequential answers never become negative findings. Deliberate negative-review shortcuts apply only to the displayed group. Routine adherence/access concerns are exception checkboxes.
- Vaccine ordering review includes age/risk, explicit immune-status assessment and prior pneumococcal products. Low-dose steroid exposure alone does not automatically establish immunocompromised vaccine eligibility. Previous PCV20/21 does not generate a routine additional pneumococcal dose. Other previous products require a history-specific schedule rather than guessed timing.
- Hidden clinical measurements and urgent findings remain effective. Inactive proposed dose schedules do not contaminate another treatment decision.
- Selected action text drives both displayed recommendations and SOAP. Completed counseling, administration, consent, normal examination, or treatment response is never invented.
- The 4-6-week default after new treatment/taper and 3-6-month stable follow-up are pragmatic review prompts, not rigid guideline mandates. Drug-specific laboratory monitoring may be earlier.
- Preview only. Clinical review and subsequent approval are required before production promotion.
