Clinical rules and source log

This platform encodes clinical guidance into structured decision support. Recommendations simplify complex evidence and may not capture patient-specific factors. SOAP notes require editing for clinical nuance. Independently verify prescriptions, monitoring and results.

This tool supports, but does not replace, clinical judgment. It does not provide emergency, organ-specific rescue, infusion-order or transplant-listing protocols. Many sarcoidosis medication regimens are off-label.
